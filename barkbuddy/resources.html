Resource FAQs

What to do if videos won't play?

Please check your internet connection or try using a different browser. If the problem persists, please contact our customer service team.

Can't open downloaded manuals?

Please ensure you have a PDF reader installed on your device. If you still can't open it, try re-downloading the file.

Need manuals in other languages?

Currently we only provide manuals in Chinese and English. If you need manuals in other languages, please contact our customer service team.

Do videos have subtitles?

Yes, all installation videos have Chinese subtitles, and some videos also provide English subtitle options.
