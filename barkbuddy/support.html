Frequently Asked Questions

Check our frequently asked questions to quickly resolve your doubts

Product Related Questions

How to choose the right aquarium size?

Choose based on your available space and the type of fish you plan to keep. Generally, small ornamental fish are suitable for 2.4 gallon aquariums, while fish that need more swimming space are better suited for smart aquariums.

How to connect the smart aquarium to WiFi?

Download the BarkBuddy APP and follow the instructions in the app to connect. Ensure your phone and aquarium are on the same WiFi network.

How to set up the automatic feeder?

Set the feeding time and amount through the APP, or use the physical buttons on the aquarium. It's recommended to adjust the feeding amount based on the type and number of fish.

Installation Related Questions

What tools are needed for aquarium installation?

Basic installation only requires a screwdriver and scissors. Complete installation may require hoses, pumps, and other accessories. Please refer to the product manual for details.

How to properly set up the filtration system?

Install the filtration system according to the instructions in the product manual, ensuring proper water flow. It's recommended to clean the filter cotton once a week and replace the filter media once a month.

Maintenance Related Questions

How to maintain aquarium water quality?

Regularly change a portion of the water (10-15% weekly), use a filter, avoid overfeeding, and clean the aquarium regularly. Smart aquariums will automatically monitor water quality and alert you.

Does the aquarium need regular cleaning?

Yes, it's recommended to clean the aquarium walls once a week and perform a thorough cleaning once a month. Be careful not to use soap or other chemical cleaners during cleaning.

Warranty Related Questions

How long is the product warranty?

Our products come with a one-year warranty from the date of purchase. During the warranty period, non-human damage failures can be repaired or replaced for free.

How to apply for warranty service?

You can contact our customer service team by phone, email, or online form, providing proof of purchase and a description of the issue, and we will arrange warranty service for you.
