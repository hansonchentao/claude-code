5 Gallon Fish Tank with Auto Feeder

Where should I place the aquarium before setup?

Place it on a flat, level, water-resistant surface with enough clearance to open the hood and access the feeder and filter. Keep it near a suitable outlet but never directly above one, and avoid direct sunlight or rapid temperature changes.

How should I prepare the tank before adding fish?

Rinse the filter sponge, filter media, substrate, plants, and decorations with clean water only. Add room-temperature dechlorinated water, make sure the pump intake is fully submerged, then run the filter for at least 24-48 hours before adding fish. Never use soap or detergent.

How do I remove the hood safely?

Keep the hood fully flat and lift it straight up. Do not try to remove it while the front panel is raised. Route the filter-pump power cord through the opening beneath the hood, and never force the hood if it does not release.

How do I set the automatic feeder?

Install the food drum securely and begin with the smallest baffle opening. Double-tap the feed button for one manual feeding. For timed feeding, press and hold the feed button for 2 seconds, then tap it to select an interval from 0-48 hours. Setting it to 0H cancels the timer. Keep the feeder dry and do not overfill the drum.

How should I clean and maintain the aquarium?

Keep the light on for no more than 8 hours per day and feed only what the fish can finish in 2-3 minutes. If the water becomes cloudy, replace about one-third at a time and never more than half. Turn off the power before a water change, clean or replace the filter sponge as needed, and clean the pump whenever you clean the aquarium.

2.4 Gallon Desktop Betta Fish Tank

How should the water pump and air tube be installed?

The submersible pump must be placed in the water, and the water level must remain above the pump motor. The oxygen pipe and air inlet valve must stay outside the aquarium and must not be submerged.

How do I prepare the water and acclimate new fish?

Condition tap water before use, then run the pump and circulate the water for 3-4 days. To acclimate a new fish, float the sealed fish bag in the aquarium for about 20 minutes. Once the bag water matches the aquarium temperature, gently release the fish.

How do I control the aquarium light?

Tap the lamp button to cycle through the automatic color-changing mode, white, dark blue, green, red, aqua blue, pink, yellow, and off. Press and hold the lamp button to adjust brightness, except while the automatic color-changing mode is active.

How do I use the built-in humidifier?

Fill the humidifier tank with purified water only - never use aquarium water. Tap the humidifier button to select P1, P2, P3, or P0 for off. The humidifier automatically stops after 4 hours; restart it manually if needed and refill the tank when empty.

What routine maintenance does the 2.4 gallon tank need?

Keep the lights on for no more than 8 hours per day and avoid overfeeding. If the water becomes cloudy, turn off the power and replace about one-third of the water at a time, never more than half. Clean or replace dirty filter cotton as needed; once a week is a typical interval depending on stocking and feeding.
