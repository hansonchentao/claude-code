About Us

Learn about BarkBuddy's story and our commitment to service

Our Story

BarkBuddy was founded in 2020 as a company dedicated to designing and manufacturing pet products. Our mission is to provide high-quality, intelligent living products for pets, allowing both pets and their owners to enjoy a better life.

Our team consists of pet-loving professionals, including designers, engineers, veterinarians, and pet behavior specialists. We are committed to creating more comfortable, healthy living environments for pets through innovative technology and human-centered design.

From our first product, the Fish Tank 2.4 Gallon, to our current Smart Fish Tank with Automatic Feeder, we have always maintained the principle of quality first, constantly improving product performance and user experience.

Service Policies

Warranty Policy

  • All products come with a one-year warranty from the date of purchase
  • During the warranty period, non-human damage failures can be repaired or replaced for free
  • Warranty coverage includes product quality issues, excluding human damage, misuse, or normal wear and tear
  • Warranty service requires proof of purchase and product serial number

Return & Exchange Policy

  • Products can be returned within 7 days of receipt if there are no quality issues
  • Returned products must be in good condition, with complete packaging and all accessories
  • Quality issues qualify for free return/exchange, while non-quality issue returns require shipping costs to be borne by the customer
  • Customized products do not support no-reason returns

Privacy Policy

  • We respect and protect users' personal information
  • Collected information is only used to provide services and improve products
  • User information will not be disclosed to third parties
  • Users can request deletion of personal information at any time

Service Commitment

  • 7×24 hour online customer support
  • Response to repair requests within 48 hours
  • Lifetime technical consultation services
  • Regular product updates and maintenance guidance
