Product FAQs

How to choose the right fish tank size?

Choose based on your space size and the type of fish you plan to keep. Generally, small ornamental fish are suitable for the 2.4 gallon tank, while fish needing more space are better suited for the smart fish tank.

How to connect the smart fish tank to WiFi?

Download the BarkBuddy app and follow the instructions in the app. Ensure your phone and fish tank are on the same WiFi network.

How to set up the automatic feeder?

Set the feeding time and amount through the app, or use the physical buttons on the fish tank. Adjust the feeding amount based on the type and number of fish.

How to maintain water quality in the fish tank?

Regularly change part of the water (10-15% weekly), use the filter, avoid overfeeding, and clean the tank regularly. The smart fish tank will automatically monitor water quality and remind you.
